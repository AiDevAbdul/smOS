## 6 · Voice & Tone

**Traits**
- Educational — explain the science, no hype
- Bold — confident in results, unafraid of bold claims backed by data
- Urgent — time is of the essence; energy and weight are quality-of-life issues
- Transparent — honest about what works, what doesn't, why

**Do**
- Lead with science and research
- Use before/afters and testimonials as proof
- Acknowledge pain points (tired, struggling, frustrated)
- Promise transformation backed by efficacy
- Use 'you' language (you'll feel, you'll transform)
- Emphasize affordability vs. alternatives

**Don't**
- Never say 'miracle,' 'guaranteed,' 'cure,' 'best ever'
- Never use fake urgency ('only 3 spots left')
- Never claim to treat or diagnose medical conditions
- Never disparage competitors or other brands
- Never use vague wellness language ('life-changing' without proof)
- Never oversell — stick to what the products deliver
