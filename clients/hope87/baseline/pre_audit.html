Wins & Gaps

Every gap is reframed as an opportunity with a cost of inaction — never a criticism.

What you're doing right
  • Instagram is a Business account categorized as "Nonprofit organization" with a linked donation page (PayPal) — the basic trust/credibility signals are already in place.
  • Facebook Page has built real community equity: 2,696 likes and 18 people actively talking about the Page after years of organic presence.
  • Website is fully responsive (mobile viewport meta present) — a baseline technical foundation is already there to build tracking on top of.
Where you're losing ground
  • No Meta Pixel, GTM, or GA4 detected anywhere on hope87.at (checked ~148KB of homepage HTML, 67 external scripts) — every visitor who clicks through to the PayPal donation link today is completely unmeasured.
  • 0 ads have ever run in the Meta Ad Library for hope87.at in Austria or Germany, while Islamic Relief Worldwide alone placed 104 ads in AT+DE in the last 90 days (~35/month, mostly carousel appeals) — you have no paid footprint at all in the two countries you most want to reach Pakistani diaspora donors.
  • Organic cadence has fallen to 0.75 posts/week (target ≥3/week for a maintained account) with an 11.6-day gap since the last post — inconsistent presence weakens trust with a diaspora audience that competitors are courting on a weekly cadence.

Recommendations

Three actions maximum. Problem → evidence → proposed action → expected outcome.

01
Every donation click is currently unmeasured.
0 tracking signals found (no fbq, no GTM, no GA4, no conversion event) across the full homepage scan.
Action: Install Meta Pixel + server-side CAPI on the PayPal/donation handoff, define a Donate conversion event.
Enables conversion-optimized ad delivery from day one instead of running blind.
02
0 ads running in Austria/Germany while a comparable NGO runs ~35 ads/month in the same countries.
Islamic Relief Worldwide: 104 ads in AT+DE over 90 days, dominant format carousel (76 of 104 ads).
Action: Structure a seasonal-appeal campaign (Ramadan/Qurbani-style moments) with Urdu/German bilingual creative targeted to the Pakistani diaspora in AT/DE.
Establishes a first paid channel in the exact target geography instead of relying on organic reach alone.
03
Posting only 0.75x/week, well below the 3x/week maintained-account benchmark.
12 most recent posts span 111.7 days (avg one post every ~9.3 days); last post was 11.6 days ago.
Action: Move to a 3x/week content rhythm mixing Reels (reach/discovery) and carousels (engagement/saves), anchored on project impact stories relevant to Pakistani donors.
Rebuilds top-of-funnel reach and warms the audience before paid spend begins.
