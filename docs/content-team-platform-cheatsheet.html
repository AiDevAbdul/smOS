Social Content Specs — 2026 Production Guide

Facebook · Instagram · TikTok · LinkedIn · YouTube

How to use this guide

This is your production reference for every post we ship. For each platform you'll find the media specs (sizes, lengths, formats), caption rules (limits + where the hook must land), and what the 2026 algorithm rewards — so every asset is built to perform, not just to fill a slot.

> Vertical-first, always. 9:16 (1080×1920) is the default for video on every platform. The hook has to land in the first 1–3 seconds or the post dies. Front-load the hook and your main keyword before the caption cuts off at "…more."

The 5 rules that apply everywhere

  1. Hook in the first 1–3 seconds. Watch-time and completion decide reach on every platform now.
  2. Front-load the caption. Every platform truncates — put the hook and primary keyword in the first ~125 characters.
  3. 3–5 hashtags, not 30. Hashtags are de-emphasized in 2026. Use a few targeted ones; stuffing does nothing (or hurts).
  4. Write for search. TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and LinkedIn are all search engines now. Keyword-rich captions, on-screen text, and alt text drive discovery as much as hashtags.
  5. Original content wins; reposting loses. Recycled/duplicated posts get suppressed (Instagram openly penalizes 10+ reposts in 30 days). Disclose AI-generated media — undisclosed AI gets down-ranked.

  • Reels: 9:16, 1080×1920, 3–90 seconds, MP4. Burn captions into the video (most people scroll with sound off).
  • Feed video: 16:9, 1:1, or 4:5.

Captions: keep it short — 40–80 characters gets the most reach. Only the first ~125 characters show before "See more." Use 1–3 hashtags max. Don't put links in the post body (it cuts reach) — put the link in the first comment.

What wins in 2026: Reels and video dominate (video is shared ~12× more than anything else). Shares and saves matter more than likes. Aim for 15–30s Reels. Post ~4–5×/week — more isn't better. Best days: Tue/Wed/Thu.

Instagram

Media sizes

  • Feed image: 4:5 — 1080 × 1350 is the default now. (Square is no longer optimal.)
  • Reels / Stories: 9:16, 1080×1920. Keep key elements inside the center (top/bottom get covered by UI).
  • Reels: up to 3 minutes. Regular feed video posts as a Reel now.

Captions: up to 2,200 characters; hook in the first ~125. Use 3–5 hashtags. Captions aren't clickable — drive to link in bio or a Stories link sticker. Always fill in alt text (helps search + accessibility).

What wins in 2026: watch time is the #1 signal, then DM shares ("sends"), then likes. Design posts people want to send to a friend — that's what reaches new audiences. Reels for reach; carousels for saves and dwell time. Keep content original — reposts get you excluded from recommendations.

TikTok

Media specs

  • 9:16, 1080 × 1920, MP4 or MOV.
  • Length: 3 seconds minimum, up to 10 min (in-app) / 60 min (upload) — but 15–30 seconds performs best.
  • Photo carousels: 2–10 images, 1080×1920.
  • Keep key elements centered (edges get covered by the UI).

Captions: up to 4,000 characters now — TikTok turned captions into an SEO tool, so pack in keywords. Only ~80–150 characters show before "…more," so front-load the hook. 3–5 hashtags. Optimal length ~150–300 characters.

What wins in 2026: completion rate — getting people to watch to the end. ~70% completion triggers the viral push, 80%+ is strong. Hook in the first 1–2 seconds. Post 3–5×/week. Ride trending audio while it's relevant. TikTok is a search engine (84% of searches are people exploring) — put keywords in your caption, on-screen text, and voiceover.

LinkedIn

Media specs

  • Images: 1200×627 (landscape), 1080×1080 (square), or 1080×1350 (portrait — best on mobile).
  • Document "carousels": upload a PDF that swipes like slides — 1080×1080 or 1080×1350, 5–10 slides is the sweet spot. (These are the single best-performing format.)
  • Video: 3 seconds–10 min; 1:1, 16:9, or 9:16.

Captions: up to 3,000 characters; optimal ~150–250 words. The "See more" cut-off is brutal — ~140 characters on mobile — so the hook has to land there. Use 3–5 hashtags.

What wins in 2026: dwell time (a 60-second read is worth ~13× a 3-second scroll) and comments (worth far more than likes). Lead with knowledge and expertise — educational beats promotional. ⚠️ Outbound links cut reach ~60%, and the old "link in first comment" trick is now penalized too — deliver the value natively and link only as a secondary step. Reply to comments fast; the first hour decides everything. Post 2–5×/week.

YouTube

Media specs

  • Shorts: 9:16, up to 3 minutes, 1080×1920. (Must be vertical and ≤3 min to land on the Shorts shelf.)
  • Long-form: 16:9, 1920×1080 or higher.
  • Thumbnail: 1280×720, under 2 MB. This is your single biggest click driver — invest in it.

Text: Title up to 100 characters (keyword in the first ~60). Description up to 5,000 characters — put the keyword in the first line. #Shorts is no longer required.

What wins in 2026: viewer satisfaction now matters more than raw watch time (YouTube measures whether people come back and keep watching). Shorts and long-form are ranked separately — for Shorts, the swipe-vs-watch decision in the first 1–3 seconds + replays + shares; for long-form, retention and click-through (thumbnail + title). Be consistent. Disclosed AI content is fine; undisclosed AI gets down-ranked.
