Cross-Platform Golden Rules

These apply to every platform, every post, every time.

Shoot vertical first. Film in 9:16 portrait (1080 x 1920). This is the default for video on every platform. Horizontal footage limits where we can use it.

Hook in the first 1-3 seconds. If the opening does not grab attention immediately, the algorithms stop showing the content. Lead with the most interesting moment.

Front-load captions. Every platform truncates text after roughly 125 characters. Put the most compelling line first -- do not bury it after filler.

Keep hashtags targeted. Use 3-5 relevant hashtags maximum. Stuffing tags is neutral at best and harmful at worst across all platforms in 2026.

Original content always wins. Every platform rewards original content and penalizes reposts. Shoot fresh footage rather than recycling clips from other channels.

Disclose AI-generated content. Any content created or substantially modified by AI tools must be labeled. Platforms enforce this, and undisclosed AI content gets removed or suppressed.

Shooting Checklist

Quick reference for your team when capturing content at the shop.

1
Shoot vertical (9:16). Hold your phone upright. All short-form video across every platform requires portrait orientation.
2
Good lighting matters. Natural light or well-lit shop bays. Avoid shooting into windows or bright doorways that blow out the image.
3
Clean audio. Reduce background noise when speaking to camera. If the shop is loud, use a clip-on microphone or move to a quieter spot.
4
Show the process. Before/during/after sequences are compelling. Film the repair in progress, the dirty vs. clean part, the finished result.
5
Keep it short. Aim for 15-30 seconds for Reels, TikTok, and Shorts. Longer content should have a clear reason to keep watching.
6
Steady your phone. Use a tripod, lean against something, or brace your elbows. Shaky footage looks unprofessional and is harder to edit.
7
Capture multiple takes. Shoot 2-3 versions of the same moment. More footage gives us more to work with in editing.
8
Get customer permission. Before filming a customer or their vehicle with identifying details, get verbal or written consent.
