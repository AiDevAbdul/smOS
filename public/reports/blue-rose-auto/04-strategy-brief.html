30-Day Launch Calendar

Week 1
Launch & Learn
  • Launch Phase A with traffic objective
  • Deploy 3 creatives across 2 audiences
  • Daily monitoring; no scaling decisions
Week 2
Optimize & Prune
  • Kill underperformers per pause thresholds
  • Refresh worst-performing creative
  • Begin reviewing audience-level signals
Week 3
Convert & Validate
  • Begin Phase B with conversion objective
  • Continue cold prospecting
  • Evaluate scaling gate (3-day ROAS check)
Week 4
Scale & Expand
  • Begin Phase C with scale objective
  • Launch 1% lookalike if gate passed
  • Plan month-2 budget and creative refresh
